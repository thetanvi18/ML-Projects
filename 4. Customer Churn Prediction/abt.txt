Customer Churn Analytics & Forecasting Platform
-An end-to-end churn intelligence system that combines machine learning, explainable AI, and time-series forecasting to predict customer churn, understand churn drivers, and forecast future churn trends for data-driven retention planning.

Project Highlights
-End-to-end churn pipeline built with Python, SQL & SQLite
-Best Model: Logistic Regression
-Model Performance: AUC = 0.84
-Explainability: SHAP for churn driver analysis
-Forecasting: SARIMA lifecycle churn forecasting
